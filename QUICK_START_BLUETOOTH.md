# Quick Start Guide: Connecting Your Bluetooth Device

## 📱 Simple 3-Step Process

### Step 1️⃣: Pair in Your Computer
**Before using the website**, pair your ESP32/Arduino device in your computer:

**Windows**: Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Add device  
**Mac**: System Preferences → Bluetooth → Connect  
**Linux**: Bluetooth settings → Pair new device  

Look for a device name like: `ESP32-Drowsiness-001` or `Arduino-Alert`

---

### Step 2️⃣: Open the Website
1. Log in to the Drowsiness Detection system
2. Click the **Bluetooth device section** in the left sidebar
3. Click the blue button: **"Recognize Connected Device"**

---

### Step 3️⃣: Select Your Device
1. A browser popup will appear showing your paired devices
2. Click on your device (e.g., "ESP32-Drowsiness-001")
3. Click **"Pair"** or **"Connect"**

**Done! ✓** Your device is now connected and saved to your account.

---

## 💡 Important Notes

✅ **Do This**: Pair device in your OS Bluetooth settings FIRST  
❌ **Don't Do This**: Try to connect in the website without pairing first  

✅ **Device must be**: Powered on and within range  
✅ **Best browsers**: Chrome, Edge, or Opera  
✅ **Connection persists**: Your device stays saved across sessions  

---

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

**Problem**: Device not appearing in browser popup?  
**Solution**: Check your OS Bluetooth settings. Is it paired and connected there?

**Problem**: "Bluetooth not available" error?  
**Solution**: Enable Bluetooth in your computer's system settings.

**Problem**: Connection fails?  
**Solution**: Try unpairing and re-pairing in your OS Bluetooth settings.

---

## 📞 Need Help?

1. Make sure device is **paired in OS first** ← Most common issue!
2. Check device is powered on and in range
3. Use Chrome browser for best compatibility
4. Restart Bluetooth service if having issues

---

**That's it!** The key is: **Pair in OS → Recognize in Website → Done!**
